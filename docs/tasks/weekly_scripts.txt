Visual `at` phrases and text fields use the narrator's exact words so actions sync to speech. Thumbnail text 3-6 true, curiosity-driving words led by a number or concrete object, `hl` = the surprise words, `visual` = the most readable picture.

6. Add new cases to data/cases.json with the next free episode numbers; files shorts/NNN-key.json. Validate, commit, push.
