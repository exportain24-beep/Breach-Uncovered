# YouTube policy notes (read 2026-10-28, support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392 and /14328491)

Monetization ("inauthentic content", formerly repetitious content, since 2025-07-15):
- NOT monetizable: template-looking / repetitive videos with little narrative or commentary, near-identical videos in a row, mass-produced feel.
- Fine: shared intro/outro and a recurring series format as long as the bulk of every video differs; AI used to edit scripts or make unique backgrounds; voice commentary with a real storyline.
=> Every episode must have its own story structure, its own archival/stock visuals, own music key (seeded by episode), different cold-open style, case-specific motion graphics. Do not publish videos that are only template + slideshow. Keep real research and sources.

Disclosure of altered/synthetic content:
- Required only for realistic content: a real person made to say/do something they did not, altered real footage, realistic fabricated scenes.
- NOT required: AI-assisted scripts, thumbnails, titles, captions, animation/unrealistic content, a clearly narrated voiceover that does not impersonate a real person.
=> Our rules (no invented dialogue, no fake footage of real people, motion graphics clearly graphic) keep us outside the disclosure requirement; `containsSyntheticMedia` is sent as false. If a future episode ever recreates realistic footage, set it true.
